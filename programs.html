Our Educational Approach

Our Proposed Programs

Making technology education accessible through community workshops, virtual learning, and personalized support.

How We Plan to Help

Education Designed Around You

Digital Bridges plans to offer free educational opportunities for adults aged 60 and older. Our programs are designed to accommodate different learning styles and accessibility needs.

🏛️

In Person Workshops

Planned: Twice per month

Educational sessions at libraries and community centers where participants can practice technology skills with volunteer assistance.

💻

Virtual Learning

Planned: Once per month

Online learning opportunities for individuals who are unable to attend in person because of transportation, mobility, or location.

🌎

Multilingual Education

Planned: Once per month

Educational sessions in additional languages to make digital literacy more accessible to diverse communities.

Practical Learning

What Participants Will Learn

Our curriculum focuses on skills that can make everyday life easier and safer.

  • Using smartphones, tablets, and computers
  • Understanding artificial intelligence
  • Scheduling appointments online
  • Recognizing scams and misinformation
  • Protecting personal information
  • Communicating with family and friends
  • Using digital services safely

Our Teaching Philosophy

Everyone learns differently. Our goal is to create a patient, supportive environment where participants feel comfortable asking questions.

Lessons will emphasize hands-on practice, simple explanations, and learning at an individual pace.

Our Expansion Plan

An Eight City Pilot Program

Digital Bridges proposes launching in eight cities across the United States, with two locations in each of four regions.

📍

Community Locations

🙋

Future Growth

Evaluate the pilot program and explore expansion into additional communities.
