Welcome to Digital Bridges

Helping older adults confidently and safely navigate an technology-driven world.

Our Purpose

Digital Bridges is dedicated to helping older adults better understand artificial intelligence and technology. Our goal is to make learning accessible, safe, and practical for everyday life.

Why Technology Literacy Matters

Artificial intelligence and Technology is becoming a part of everyday life. Understanding how AI works can help older adults recognize scams, protect personal information, and confidently use new technology.

Learning Together

We believe learning technology should be welcoming and accessible to everyone. Our educational programs focus on building confidence through practical experience and guided instruction.

How We Help

AI Education

We help older adults understand the basics of technology and how AI tools can be useful in everyday situations.

Online Safety

We focus on recognizing AI-related scams, identifying misinformation, and protecting personal information online.

Hands-On Support

Our goal is to provide guided learning opportunities where individuals can practice using technology and AI tools with assistance.
