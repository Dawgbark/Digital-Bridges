Why Digital Bridges?

Technology Is Changing. Support Should Keep Pace.

Many older adults face challenges adapting to rapidly changing technology. Confusing instructions, privacy concerns, and limited access to in-person support can create barriers.

Digital Bridges aims to close this gap through accessible education, practical learning, and community-based support.
