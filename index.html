Our Purpose

Bridging the Digital Divide

Technology should bring people together, not leave them behind. Digital Bridges is a proposed nonprofit dedicated to making technology education accessible, practical, and welcoming.

💡

AI Education

Learn what artificial intelligence is and how it can help with everyday tasks, from writing messages to finding useful information.

🛡️

Online Safety

Build skills to recognize scams, protect personal information, and identify misleading online content.

🤝

Personal Support

Practice technology skills through patient instruction, supportive volunteers, and community learning.

Why It Matters

Technology Is Changing. Everyone Deserves to Keep Up.

Artificial intelligence is becoming part of healthcare, communication, financial services, and daily activities. Yet many older adults face barriers to learning new tools.

Digital Bridges aims to reduce these barriers through accessible education, hands-on experience, and opportunities to learn together.
