Technology Resources

Simple resources to help individuals understand and safely use artificial intelligence in everyday life.

Understanding Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence is technology that allows computers to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Examples include voice assistants, online recommendations, translation tools, and AI chatbots.

Staying Safe with AI

As artificial intelligence becomes more advanced, it is important to understand how to protect yourself online.

Online Safety Tips

  • Never share passwords or personal financial information with AI tools.
  • Be cautious of suspicious emails, messages, and phone calls.
  • Verify information before trusting or sharing it.
  • Use strong passwords and secure websites.

Learning to Use AI Tools

AI tools can help with everyday tasks such as writing emails, finding information, and learning new skills.
