Online Safety

Protect Yourself While Using Technology

Digital literacy includes knowing how to recognize risks and protect your personal information.

  • Use strong, unique passwords
  • Never share verification codes
  • Be cautious of suspicious messages
  • Verify information before sharing it
  • Avoid unexpected links and attachments
  • Ask a trusted person when unsure

Remember

Scammers may impersonate businesses, family members, or government organizations.

If someone unexpectedly asks for money or private information, stop and verify the request using a trusted contact method.

Beginner Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Select a question to view the answer.

What is an AI chatbot?

An AI chatbot is a computer program that can respond to questions and messages. It can explain topics, suggest ideas, and help with everyday tasks.

How can AI help me write an email?

You can ask an AI tool to create a draft email. Review the response, correct any mistakes, and personalize it before sending.

How can I recognize a scam?

Watch for urgent requests, suspicious links, unusual sender addresses, and demands for personal information. Verify unexpected requests independently.

Can I trust everything AI tells me?

No. AI can make mistakes and provide inaccurate information. Always verify important information with reliable sources.

How do I protect my privacy?

Use strong passwords, enable multifactor authentication, and avoid sharing sensitive information with unfamiliar services.
