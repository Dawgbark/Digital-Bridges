Protect Yourself

Staying Safe Online

Understanding online safety is one of the most important parts of digital literacy.

🔒

Protect Personal Information

Never share passwords, verification codes, or sensitive financial information with unknown individuals or AI chatbots.

⚠️

Recognize Scams

Be cautious of unexpected messages, suspicious links, and urgent requests for money or personal information.

✓

Verify Information

AI tools can provide incorrect information. Always verify important facts using reliable sources.

Beginner Guides

Common Technology Questions

Select a question below to learn more.

What is an AI chatbot?

An AI chatbot is a computer program that responds to questions and messages. It can help explain topics, generate ideas, and assist with everyday tasks.

How can AI help me write an email?

You can ask an AI tool to help create a friendly or professional email. Review the response and make changes before sending it.

How do I recognize a suspicious email?

Look for unexpected links, urgent demands, unusual sender addresses, and requests for private information. Contact the organization directly if you are unsure.

Can I trust everything AI tells me?

No. AI can make mistakes or generate incorrect information. Verify important medical, financial, and legal information with trusted professionals or sources.

How do I protect my privacy online?

Use strong, unique passwords, enable multifactor authentication when available, and avoid sharing sensitive information on unfamiliar websites.
