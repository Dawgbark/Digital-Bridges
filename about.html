About Digital Bridges

Learn more about our mission, our goals, and why Digital Bridges was created.

Our Mission

Our mission is to improve technology and artificial intelligence literacy among older adults by providing accessible educational opportunities that promote safe and responsible technology use.

Why We Started

As artificial intelligence becomes more common in everyday life, many older adults face challenges understanding and adapting to new technologies.

Digital Bridges was created to help close this knowledge gap and provide individuals with the confidence and skills needed to navigate an increasingly digital world.

Our Goals

Increase Technology Literacy

Help older adults understand artificial intelligence and how to realte that with ever changing technology used in practical everyday situations.

Promote Safe AI Use

Teach individuals how to recognize AI-related scams, misinformation, and potential privacy risks.

Build Confidence

Provide supportive learning environments where individuals can develop confidence using AI and emerging technologies.
