Who We Are

About Digital Bridges

Building a future where older adults have the knowledge, resources, and confidence to navigate technology.

Our Mission

Making Technology Accessible to Everyone

Our mission is to improve technology and artificial intelligence literacy among older adults by providing accessible educational opportunities that promote safe and responsible technology use.

We believe age should never be a barrier to understanding and benefiting from modern technology.

Who We Serve

Digital Bridges is designed primarily for adults aged 60 and older who want to strengthen their digital skills and become more comfortable using technology.

Our educational approach also welcomes individuals with different experience levels and learning needs.

The Challenge

Why Digital Bridges Was Created

As artificial intelligence becomes more common, older adults may encounter challenges understanding and adapting to rapidly changing technology.

The Confidence Gap

Research from organizations such as Pew Research Center highlights differences in technology and AI adoption across age groups.

Limited Accessibility

Some educational programs may be difficult to access because of location, transportation, technology requirements, or limited availability.

The Need for Support

Many learners benefit from patient instruction, practical demonstrations, and opportunities to ask questions in person.

Our Goals

What We Hope to Accomplish

📱

Increase Digital Literacy

Teach practical technology skills for communication, scheduling appointments, using devices, and navigating online services.

🔒

Promote Online Safety

Help participants recognize scams, misinformation, privacy risks, and unsafe online interactions.

🌱

Build Lasting Confidence

Create supportive environments where individuals can learn at their own pace and practice new skills.

Our Difference

Combining Education and Community

Digital Bridges is designed to complement existing programs by offering multiple ways to learn.

Our proposed approach combines community-based workshops, virtual sessions, multilingual learning, and volunteer support.

Supporting Educational Equity

Our initiative supports the broader goals of United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 4: Quality Education.

We aim to promote lifelong learning and expand access to useful digital skills for older adults.
