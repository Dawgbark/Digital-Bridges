Our Story

About Digital Bridges

A community-centered approach to closing the digital literacy gap.

Our Mission

Build Skills. Build Trust. Build Community.

Digital Bridges aims to improve technology and artificial intelligence literacy among older adults through accessible education that promotes safe, responsible, and practical technology use.

Our goal is to help people confidently use digital tools for communication, scheduling appointments, everyday tasks, and informed decision-making.

Our Priorities

What Guides Our Work

✦

Digital Confidence

◇

♡

Design instruction around individual needs, accessibility, and community support.

The Bigger Picture

Education at Every Age

Our proposal supports lifelong learning and educational inclusion, consistent with the broad goals of United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 4: Quality Education.

Digital Bridges is a student-developed nonprofit concept. We have not launched classes or established pilot locations.

Research Informed

Our proposal draws on research and educational initiatives from Pew Research Center, AARP Senior Planet, the National Council on Aging, and EY.

Our goal is to address barriers related to accessibility, confidence, and practical learning.
