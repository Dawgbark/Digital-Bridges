Our Story

About Digital Bridges

A community-centered approach to closing the digital literacy gap.

Our Mission

Build Skills. Build Trust. Build Community.

Digital Bridges aims to improve technology and artificial intelligence literacy among older adults through accessible education that promotes safe, responsible, and practical technology use.

Our goal is to help people confidently use digital tools for communication, scheduling appointments, everyday tasks, and informed decision-making.

Our Priorities

What Guides Our Work

Our approach focuses on making technology education accessible, safe, and welcoming.

✦

Digital Confidence

Make unfamiliar devices and AI tools feel more approachable through practical education and support.

◇

Safety First

Focus on scams, privacy, misinformation, and responsible technology use.

♡

People First

Design instruction around individual needs, accessibility, and community support.

Our Leadership Approach

Leadership That Puts People First

Digital Bridges is guided by two leadership approaches that emphasize understanding, supporting, and empowering older adults.

♡

Servant Leadership

Prioritizes the needs, concerns, and well-being of older adults through accessible education, patient instruction, and community support.

Eva et al., 2019
◎

Human-Centered Leadership

Develops educational programs around participants' experiences, individual learning needs, and technological challenges.

Harvard Business Impact, 2024

Stronger Technology Literacy for Older Adults

By combining servant leadership with a human-centered approach, Digital Bridges aims to create an educational environment where older adults feel supported, respected, and confident learning new technologies.

The Bigger Picture

Education at Every Age

Our proposal supports lifelong learning and educational inclusion, consistent with the broad goals of United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 4: Quality Education.

Digital Bridges is a student-developed nonprofit concept. We have not launched classes or established pilot locations.

Research Informed

Our proposal draws on research and educational initiatives from Pew Research Center, AARP Senior Planet, the National Council on Aging, and EY.

Our goal is to address barriers related to accessibility, confidence, and practical learning.
